# Dawnglass – Days Counter: update log

What changes in each version of the app and its policies, in plain words.

## App versions

### 1.1.0 — in testing
- Idle buddy: when your home screen sits idle for a while, Tikku hops and says hello — at most
  twice a day, never in battery saver. Switch it off in Wallpaper settings.
- 22 more wallpaper scenes, grouped by mood (29 in all), and four looks for Tikku: Classic,
  Scholar, Monk and Champ.
- Weekly plan: one short line for each day of the week (like a subject a day), in a small notepad
  box in the daily planner — repeat it every week or plan just this week — and a "Weekly plan"
  home-screen widget.
- Lock-screen timer: set a timer (for example 2 or 3 hours) and see the time left on your lock
  screen. Optional "Screen always on": a big countdown (with an optional dial) stays on screen,
  also over the lock screen, until the time is up.
- Finished countdowns step aside: from the day after, the wallpaper and widgets show your next
  countdown instead of counting days backwards; finished ones wait in a "Done" section in the
  app, where "Clear" moves them to the archive.

### 1.0.2
- Finished countdowns step aside for your next one instead of showing "1 day ago" (also in 1.1).

### 1.0.1
- Fixed: tapping an app on your home screen or in the app drawer could open the clock instead.

### 1.0.0 — first public version (in testing)
- Tapping the clock on the live wallpaper now opens your phone's clock app itself (alarms,
  world clock, stopwatch, timer), like tapping a clock widget — before, some phones showed only
  a bare alarm list.
- Smoother app: the animated wallpaper preview now runs at the same calm pace as the wallpaper
  itself, your own photo loads without freezing the screen, and screens open faster.

### 0.1.0 — in testing (not yet on Google Play)
The first version.
- A home screen with everything one tap away: countdowns, the daily planner, wallpapers,
  adding a widget, reports and settings, plus a moon button for dark mode and a star button
  for the quotes you starred on the widget. Every feature starts
  on; turn off what you don't need.
- Countdowns for exams, trips, birthdays and deadlines: the days left in big numbers,
  with a live clock below.
- Two home-screen widgets: your countdowns, and your countdowns with a quote of the day. In
  both, swipe the countdown up or down to see the others; small dots show which one you are
  on. In Settings you can turn the small live clock on widgets off, and choose to see the
  edges of the next cards like a deck. Both show your next planned task (tap it to open today's plan) and a button for the
  motivation playlist. Both fit every phone, tablet and foldable, at every text size.
- Live wallpaper: seven animated scenes or your own photo, with a power-saver mode. Your
  countdown and the quote of the day are built into the wallpaper (on by default; each can be
  turned off), with your next planned task underneath. Choose a days box beside the quote or
  the days above it. Power saver keeps the countdown and quote, only the animation stops.
  A clock sits at the top in a style you choose (Classic, Big, Bold, Elegant or Pill), a
  little higher or lower if you like (tap it to open your alarms), and round buttons under the panel let
  you switch countdowns, get a new quote or open the motivation playlist; tap your next task
  to open today's plan. The clock and the buttons can each be turned off.
- Optional reminder when a countdown reaches zero, and optional on-time updates so widgets
  change exactly at midnight.
- Daily planner: an hourly plan for the day (tap an hour to add a task — the time is filled
  in, just type the name; remove a task with the bin icon). Add tasks with just a name and a time, tick them off, and get a weekly score
  with encouragement and a motivation video button. A monthly report you can share as a
  picture, with stars for how your month went. Two looks: Paper and Night.
- Optional check-in notifications ask whether you finished a task, and the live wallpaper can
  show your next task.
- Privacy: no internet access, no account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone
  and "Delete all my data" erases it.

## Policy versions

### 23 November 2026 — Privacy Policy version 1.4
- Adds the lock-screen timer: while a timer runs, the App keeps when it ends, whether to keep the
  screen on and whether to show the dial, on your phone only. The lock screen shows only the time left. Nothing
  is collected or sent.

### 20 November 2026 — Privacy Policy version 1.3
Lists two more things the App keeps on your phone for version 1.1: your weekly plan lines, and
when Tikku last said hello (so it happens at most twice a day). Both stay on your phone, are
covered by "Hide titles outside the app" and "Delete all my data", and are never sent anywhere.

### 20 November 2026 — Privacy Policy version 1.2
Tapping the clock on the live wallpaper now opens your phone's clock app instead of only its
alarm list. To find the clock app, the App asks Android which app handles alarms; it cannot see
your other apps. The permission is the same one as before, and the App still never sets, reads
or changes your alarms. Nothing else changed.

### 16 November 2026 — Privacy Policy version 1.1
Adds one permission to the list: opening your phone's alarm list when you tap the clock on the
live wallpaper. It is granted automatically, and the App never sets, reads or changes your
alarms. Nothing else changed.

### 15 November 2026 — Privacy Policy and Terms of Use, version 1.0
First published versions, including the daily planner and sharing your monthly report.
